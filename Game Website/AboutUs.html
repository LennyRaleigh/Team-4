About Us

Our Vision

Our goal is to create a fun and engaging 2D RPG puzzle game using Python and the Pygame library.

Originally, we were unsure about the theme and even the genre of our game. After exploring different ideas, we decided to create a farming-themed RPG puzzle game where players solve puzzles and progress through an exciting adventure.

The game will feature 3 levels with increasing difficulty. Each level introduces new mechanics, enemies, puzzles, and environments to keep the gameplay fresh and challenging.

Our game will also include a simple main menu with an overview of the game, buttons to access each level, and a settings menu featuring mute and character selection options.
