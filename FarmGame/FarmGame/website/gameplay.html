HOW TO PLAY

Controls, mechanics, tips — everything you need to keep Borris alive.

CONTROLS

Every action Borris can perform, mapped to your keyboard.

KEY ACTION NOTES
W Move Up Borris runs north across the farm
A Move Left Sprite flips to face left automatically
S Move Down Borris runs south across the farm
D Move Right Sprite faces right automatically
SPACE Dodge Roll Speed burst — invincible during dodge frames. 60-frame cooldown.
ENTER Attack Melee strike in facing direction. 15 dmg, 30-frame cooldown.
R Restart Only active on the Game Over screen.
ESC Quit Exits the game from the Game Over screen.

MECHANICS

CARROT COLLECTION

Carrots are placed around each level at fixed positions. Simply walk Borris into a carrot to collect it. A glowing aura surrounds each carrot to help you spot them. The HUD in the top-left shows your current tally . Collect all to progress.

ENEMY AI

The three foxes always know where Borris is and chase him using steering vectors. They also push each other apart to avoid clumping, making them harder to evade in a group. Contact with a fox drains 5 HP per hit, subject to a 30-frame invincibility window. A fox's health bar appears above it once it takes any damage.

⚡

DODGE & ATTACK COOLDOWNS

Both the dodge and attack have cooldowns measured in game frames at 60 FPS. The dodge cooldown is 60 frames (~1 second) and lasts for 15 frames. The attack lasts 12 frames with a 30-frame cooldown. Timing your dodge to pass through a fox while it can't retaliate is the key skill in this game.

TIPS & TRICKS

🏃

KEEP MOVING

Standing still is a death sentence. The foxes never stop chasing you — constant movement makes it harder for them to corner Borris.

🎯

ATTACK DIRECTION

Borris attacks in the direction he's currently facing. Face a fox, then press ENTER. The hit-box appears at the front edge of Borris with a 55-pixel reach.

💨

DODGE THROUGH FOXES

During a dodge roll Borris is completely invincible. Use it to burst through a fox that's blocking your path to a carrot — you won't take any damage while dodging.

🗺️

MEMORISE CARROT SPOTS

Carrots always spawn at the same seven positions. Learn the layout and plan a route that minimises time spent near foxes.

❤️

WATCH THE HUD

Your green health bar sits in the top-left corner. Keep an eye on it — 7 un-dodged fox contacts is all it takes to end the run.

🦊

FIGHT OR FLEE?

Each fox has 100 HP and you deal 15 per hit — that's 7 hits to kill one. Fighting is slower than dodging, but clearing a fox permanently opens up the farm. Your call.

WIN & LOSE CONDITIONS

🏆

WIN

Collect all 7 carrots before Borris's health reaches zero. The counter in the HUD reaches 7/7 and you've beaten the round.

💀

LOSE

Borris's HP drops to zero from fox contact. The Game Over screen shows how many carrots you collected. Press R to try again.
