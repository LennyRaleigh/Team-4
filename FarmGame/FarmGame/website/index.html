WHAT IS THIS GAME?

Borris the Bunny is a top-down action game built with Python and Pygame. Guide Borris across a pixel-art farm, collect every carrot, and fend off enemies to save your family from the farmer.

🥕

COLLECT CARROTS

Golden carrots are hidden across the farm. Grab them all to progress through each level.

🦊

DODGE THE ENEMIES

Enemies chase you down with no mercy. Use SPACE to burst-dodge out of trouble before your HP hits zero.

⚔️

FIGHT BACK

Press ENTER to unleash a melee strike. Each hit deals 15 damage — enough to take down a fox in just seven blows.

🏡

PIXEL FARM WORLD

Explore a hand-crafted pixel-art farm with animated characters, a scrolling backdrop, and satisfying crunch-and-dodge combat.

MEET THE CAST

BORRIS THE BUNNY

Our hero. An 8-frame animated rabbit with a need for speed and an appetite for carrots. He can run in all four directions, dodge on a short cooldown, and punch foxes in the snout. Borris starts with 100 HP — protect him at all costs.

THE FOXES

Three cunning foxes with a 4-frame prowl animation. They always know where Borris is and actively steer toward him while avoiding each other with separation logic. Each contact drains 5 HP. They flash white when hit, and a red health bar appears above them once damaged.

READY TO PLAY?

Run python code/main.py from the project root to launch the game. Make sure you have Python 3 and Pygame installed.
